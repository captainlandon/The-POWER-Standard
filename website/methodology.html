Public method

Show the evidence. Preserve the uncertainty.

POWER is designed to strengthen accountability without inventing certainty, treating correlation as causation, or judging an office for powers it does not possess.

Four governing principles

Authority before accountability

Begin with the legal and institutional powers of the relevant actor.

Evidence before judgment

Separate source material, factual claims, analysis, and conclusions.

Unknown is a valid answer

Display missing, conflicting, or insufficient evidence openly.

Influence before participation

Show where and when public action can affect a decision.

Evidence model

Every important assertion should be traceable to one or more sources and represented with enough context to understand what the source can and cannot establish.

ElementRequirement
SourcePublisher, author when available, title, date, URL or archival locator, access date, and document type.
ClaimA precise proposition linked to the portion of a source that supports, contradicts, or qualifies it.
ProvenanceWhether the material was submitted, verified, compiled, imported, transcribed, calculated, or inferred.
ConfidenceA reasoned statement about completeness and reliability, not a decorative percentage.
DisagreementCompeting sources or interpretations remain inspectable.
Correction historyChanges preserve the prior state, reason, author, and time.

No single accountability score

A universal score would conceal differences between authority, effort, implementation, outcomes, causation, cooperation, obstruction, and changed conditions. POWER should instead present a structured record with dimension-specific findings and plain-language explanations. Comparisons may be appropriate when the underlying concepts and evidence are truly comparable.

Known and unknown

Knowledge statePOWER treatment
Known knownDocument the claim, source, scope, date, and relevant context.
Known unknownName the missing fact or unresolved question and why it matters.
Unknown knownIdentify information that exists but is fragmented, inaccessible, overlooked, or held by another actor.
Unknown unknownPreserve space for discovery, revision, external challenge, and unexpected consequences.

Safeguards

  • Right of response for candidates, offices, agencies, and materially affected people
  • Visible distinction between source fact, analysis, forecast, signal, and allegation
  • Public correction and appeal pathways
  • Version history that prevents silent rewriting of commitments
  • Privacy protection for individuals and location-based personalization
  • Accessibility, plain language, machine-readable exports, and reproducible methods
  • No declaration of illegality without an authoritative legal finding

Stewardship and governance

The Tao Institute for Actualization is the intended steward. A durable public instrument will also need independent methodological review, conflict disclosures, contributor standards, data governance, security practices, a maintenance or responsible-retirement plan, and a defined boundary between charitable public-interest work and any future earned-revenue activity.

Methodology is a living public document. Material revisions should be versioned and explained.
