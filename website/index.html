A civic standard, not a score

Make the full chain of public responsibility visible.

The POWER Standard is a nonpartisan framework and civic technology platform for turning political claims and public plans into structured, sourced, revisable records.

It does not reduce democracy to a single grade. It preserves context, uncertainty, changes, disagreements, and responsibility so the public can see what was promised, what was possible, what happened, and what remains unknown.

What it changes

Campaign language, budgets, legislation, agency action, public data, and community experience stop living as isolated systems. POWER links them without pretending they are equivalent.

Methodological guardrails

Accountability without false certainty.

POWER is designed to be rigorous enough for public scrutiny and humble enough to show what the evidence cannot establish.

Authority before accountability

Judge an actor in relation to the powers, duties, and constraints of the office.

Evidence before judgment

Separate claims, sources, interpretation, and conclusions.

Unknown is a valid answer

Missing or conflicting evidence is shown, not quietly converted into certainty.

Influence before participation

Participation is meaningful only when people can see where decisions can actually change.

Stewardship

The Tao Institute for Actualization

POWER is a public-interest initiative of the Tao Institute for Actualization. The Institute studies and builds conditions that help people, communities, and institutions move from latent capacity toward responsible flourishing.

A future Flourishing Outcomes layer may help examine whether public action improves the conditions of human and ecological life. It should remain transparent, plural, revisable, and separate from the core factual record.

Integrity commitments
  • Source labels, provenance, uncertainty, and correction history
  • Versioned public plans that preserve original commitments
  • Community evidence alongside administrative and research data
  • Accessible exports and open, reusable technical work where possible
  • Privacy separation for personal civic context and analytics

Help build a public record worthy of democracy.

POWER is in active design and prototype development. We welcome problem owners, public servants, civic technologists, researchers, community organizations, candidates, and careful critics.
