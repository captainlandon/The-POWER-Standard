# POWER Standard site audit

Audit date: 2026-10-29

## Purpose

This release reconciles the recent POWER Standard working conversations into a coherent public website. It distinguishes the civic standard, public platform, proposed pilot, historical research module, and related Tao Institute instruments.

## Decisions reflected in the site

- Professional name: The POWER Standard
- Public expansion: Public Office Work, Evidence and Results
- Public slogan: Public power should leave a public record.
- Primary mark: the Open Civic Seal
- Civic framing: nonpartisan, evidence-aware, public-interest infrastructure
- Core chain: Problem, Authority, Public Actor, Plan or Commitment, Resources, Implementation, Outcome, Evidence
- Core principles: Authority Before Accountability; Evidence Before Judgment; Unknown Is a Valid Answer; Influence Before Participation
- Initial product focus: POWER Plan Builder, Plan Comparison, candidate notice or response, and a thin Mandate Ledger
- Candidate participation: direct submission and verification are invited, but clearly labeled POWER-compiled records may be created from public statements
- Major party imagery: donkey and elephant remain prominent, paired with one common standard and a broader American Political Parties primer
- Historical accountability: POWER Retrospective is a distinct research module, with the National Housing Act of 1934 as the proposed first pilot
- Related instruments: Panoptica, Prism, POWER, and Compass remain distinct but interoperable
- Flourishing: retained as a future transparent, plural, revisable outcomes layer, not folded into the factual core
- Signals: potential unethical or illegal behavior indicators are framed as investigatory signals, not findings of guilt

## Site changes

- Rebuilt the homepage around the actual accountability standard and its public value
- Added a transparent prototype status banner and limitations notice
- Added platform architecture, methodology, and retrospective pages
- Preserved and linked the American Political Parties civic primer
- Added explicit module maturity labels such as Pilot focus, Prototype, Designed, Future module, and Research concept
- Added candidate participation and POWER-compiled record rules
- Added provenance, uncertainty, correction, right-of-response, privacy, and causal safeguards
- Added the Tao Institute stewardship and Flourishing Outcomes relationship
- Added the wider civic instrument flow: Analyze, Debate, Commit, Measure, Reassess
- Linked the open GitHub repository

## Important product boundary

The public site is an explanatory working prototype. It does not yet contain a production government-data pipeline, live candidate records, jurisdiction lookup, automated auditing, legal determinations, or a validated Flourishing Index. Illustrative content is labeled accordingly.

## Suggested next build sequence

1. Finalize the Plan Builder schema and submission or compilation workflow.
2. Implement candidate notice, verification, response, correction, and version history.
3. Build one jurisdiction-specific authority map and one issue taxonomy.
4. Publish side-by-side plan comparison and a thin Mandate Ledger.
5. Add durable provenance-aware storage, accessibility testing, exports, and privacy controls.
6. Run a separate Retrospective research pilot.
