# Civic Tech DC | Three Civic Technology Projects

**Innovatio et Cetera · Anier Woodyard · Washington, DC**

This is a public introduction to three projects being developed within the Innovatio portfolio. The descriptions below are invitations to explore and collaborate, not claims that all features are deployed or production-ready.

## 1. The POWER Standard — public accountability

**Public power should leave a public record.**

POWER is a nonpartisan civic accountability framework and developing platform connecting public problems, institutional authority, commitments, plans, resources, implementation, evidence, and measurable results.

- **Explore the public code and documentation:** [The POWER Standard](https://github.com/captainlandon/The-POWER-Standard)
- **Public-facing project website:** [The POWER Standard website](https://power-standard-home.bandolando28.chatgpt.site)
- **Suggested Civic Tech DC pilot:** Trace one documented DC policy commitment across authority, public plans, resources, implementation evidence, and reported results.
- **Looking for:** public-policy researchers, civic data contributors, UX designers, accessibility testers, and developers.

## 2. Panoptica — systems of power and harm

Panoptica explores how to make institutional power, rules, and their consequences more legible. Its research-driven approach seeks to examine source materials such as policies and institutional documents, trace relationships, and produce explainable, evidence-linked maps of power and potential harm.

- **Development status:** active exploration; source repository remains private pending a public-release and security review.
- **Potential collaborators:** critical researchers, community practitioners, designers, and engineers interested in responsible civic intelligence.
- **Relationship to POWER:** Panoptica examines the structures surrounding decisions; POWER traces commitments, implementation, and results.

## 3. Prism — evidence-centered public discourse

Prism is an AI-assisted platform for structured debate, critical thinking, evidence-based research, and collaborative reasoning. Concepts under development include argument analysis, potential fallacy detection, source-backed fact-checking, and different modes for social, educational, and civic deliberation.

- **Development status:** working concepts and code in private repositories; public release requires further readiness review.
- **Potential collaborators:** debate facilitators, educators, fact-checking researchers, frontend engineers, and responsible-AI practitioners.
- **Civic potential:** more substantive policy forums and structured public deliberation.
